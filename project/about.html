How It Started

A gift that missed the mark.

In 2019, co-founder Jamie bought his dad — a lifelong club player — a generic sports voucher for his birthday. His dad smiled politely. Everyone knew it wasn't quite right.

Jamie spent weeks looking for something more personal: a beautiful tennis print, a custom ball tin, something that felt like it was made for a tennis person. He couldn't find it.

So he made it himself. Tennis Gift launched with four products and a simple promise: every item should feel worthy of wrapping up and handing over with pride.

"Every gift should feel like it means something."

Jamie, Co-Founder

What We Stand For

Our Values

Curation Over Volume

We stock fewer products than most shops — but every single one has been personally tested and approved. Quality always beats quantity.

Gift-First Thinking

Everything we do — from packaging to product descriptions — is designed with the gift-giver in mind. We make the experience delightful, not just functional.

Fast, Reliable Shipping

Birthdays don't wait. Most orders ship within 24 hours. We'll never leave you scrambling for a last-minute gift.

For Every Level of Fan

You don't need to know the difference between a topspin forehand and a slice to shop here. If someone loves tennis, we have something for them.

Sustainable Packaging

All our packaging is recyclable. Our gift boxes are made from FSC-certified board and printed with water-based inks.

No-Quibble Returns

Not right? No problem. 30-day returns, no questions asked. We'd rather you find the perfect gift, even if that means sending it back.
